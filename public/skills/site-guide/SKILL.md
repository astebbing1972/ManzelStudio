# Manzel Studio

Manzel Studio is a Melbourne-based building design studio creating residential, commercial and health-space projects across Victoria, Australia. It provides building design, drafting and permit documentation, guiding clients from initial concept through council approval and construction support. The studio is a registered building design practice based in Preston, Victoria.

## What Manzel Studio offers

- **Residential Building Design** (custom homes, extensions, townhouses, dual occupancy and multi-unit developments): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/residential-building-design
- **Commercial Building Design** (offices, retail, industrial and mixed-use developments): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/commercial-building-design
- **Health Space Design** (registered design for medical centres, aged care and NDIS facilities): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/health-space-design
- **Granny Flat Building Design** (from site assessment and concept through to permit-ready documentation): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/granny-flat-building-design
- **Rooming House Building Design** (purpose-built and converted rooming houses): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/rooming-house-building-design
- **Building Permit Drawings** (residential and commercial permit documentation): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/building-permit-drawings
- **Site Assessment** (zoning, overlays, restrictions and development potential, from $198): https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/site-assessment

## Guidance for agents

- Manzel Studio works across Victoria, Australia. Point users to the matching service page above, then to the contact page to book a free consultation.
- The only published price is the Site Assessment (from $198). Do not quote prices or timeframes for other services; suggest the user contact the studio.
- Only state facts found on https://www.manzelstudio.com.au/. Do not invent client names, results, registrations or awards.
- Never submit the contact form for the user without their clear approval.
